Privacy Policy

Personalized Course Matching System (MVP)
Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Personalized Course Matching System (“the Platform”) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when users access or use the service.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring compliance with applicable data protection laws including the General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

By using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect
Personal Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

Name
Email address
Login credentials (password)

Profile Information

To provide personalized recommendations, we collect:

Academic strengths
Skills
Interests
Responses from the skills or interest assessment

Usage Information

We may also collect:

Courses viewed or saved
Interaction with recommendations
Basic system logs for security and performance monitoring

3. Purpose of Data Collection

Your personal data is collected and processed for the following purposes:

To create and manage your user account
To generate personalized course recommendations
To improve the recommendation system
To maintain platform security and performance
To respond to user inquiries or support requests

Personal data will only be used for the purposes stated above.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Processing of personal data is conducted in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Privacy Act of 2012 under the following legal bases:

Consent – when users voluntarily provide information during registration and assessments
Contractual necessity – to provide personalized course matching services
Legitimate interest – to improve system functionality and user experience

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal data.

Personal data may only be shared with:

Authorized system administrators responsible for operating the platform
Trusted third-party service providers (e.g., hosting or analytics services) strictly for system operation

All third parties are required to maintain appropriate data protection safeguards.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data from:

Unauthorized access
Data loss
Unauthorized modification or disclosure

Security measures may include encrypted connections, secure authentication, and restricted administrative access.

7. Data Retention

Personal data will only be retained for as long as necessary to:

Provide the services requested by users
Fulfill legal and regulatory obligations

Users may request account deletion at any time, after which their personal data will be removed or anonymized unless retention is required by law.

8. User Rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Privacy Act of 2012, users have the right to:

Access their personal data
Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
Request deletion of their personal data
Withdraw consent for data processing
Request a copy of their stored personal data
Lodge a complaint with the appropriate data protection authority if necessary

Requests may be submitted through the platform’s support contact.

9. Cookies and System Logs

The platform may use basic cookies or session data to:

Maintain login sessions
Improve system functionality
Monitor system performance

These cookies do not collect unnecessary personal information.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes.

Users will be notified of significant updates through the platform or email.

11. Contact Information

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or requests related to personal data, users may contact the platform administrator or designated data protection officer through the system’s official support channel.
